Dr. Elena Morris

Department: Computer Science

Position: Senior Lecturer

Dr. Morris teaches introductory programming and software development. Her academic interests include problem-solving, programming fundamentals, and accessible digital learning.

Courses: Introduction to Computer Science and software development.

Dr. James Wilson

Department: Mathematics

Position: Lecturer

Dr. Wilson teaches undergraduate mathematics and helps students develop analytical reasoning skills. His teaching interests include calculus, applied mathematics, and mathematical modeling.

Courses: Calculus and applied mathematics.

Prof. Sophia Bennett

Department: Languages and Communication

Position: Associate Professor

Professor Bennett supports students in academic writing, communication, and English language development. Her teaching emphasizes clear expression, critical reading, and effective presentation skills.

Courses: Academic English and communication skills.

Prof. Michael Turner

Department: Business Administration

Position: Senior Lecturer

Professor Turner teaches business principles, management, and organizational strategy. His classes introduce students to decision-making, teamwork, and practical business cases.

Courses: Business management and organizational behavior.
